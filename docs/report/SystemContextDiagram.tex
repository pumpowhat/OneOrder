\# & Interaction & What flows \\ \thead
1 & Contractor → Sales desk & The order arrives \emph{outside} OneOrder as an email, a photo or an Excel file. There is no contractor portal in the MVP. \\ \trow
\hl 2 & \hl Sales desk → OneOrder & \hl Imports a sample email, uploads a file or pastes text; fixes flagged lines and types a missing customer name; approves; chooses delivery site; confirms or cancels (HTTPS, browser). \\ \trow
3 & OneOrder → Sales desk & Draft order with confidence per line, stock pre-check, fulfilment plan with the reason for each source, ETA per shipment, late and shortage warnings. \\ \trow
4 & OneOrder → AI provider & The photo, the email text or the Excel columns, plus catalogue candidates for hard-to-match lines. \\ \trow
5 & AI provider → OneOrder & Structured JSON: customer, delivery site, requested date, lines with quantity, unit, description and confidence. \\ \trow
6 & ERPs → OneOrder & Stock per warehouse in each ERP's own format, normalised by one adapter per country. \\ \trow
7 & OneOrder → ERPs & A reservation per shipment line, written when the sales desk confirms. Later orders then see the stock as reserved. \\ \trow
8 & OneOrder ↔ Salesforce (DK) & Suggest customer at Review. After Confirm, write the order through the CRM adapter; on failure leave it queued for retry. Dynamics exports only into the migration path. \\
\end{tabularx}}
